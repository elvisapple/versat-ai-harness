# Contribution Standard

Status: private H02 implementation candidate; not yet adopted or released.

## Entry and precedence

- A contributor MUST use the intended repository's actual issue forms, setup, contribution requirements and review/check process. Repository-specific guidance takes precedence over organization defaults.
- Organization defaults MUST retain a concise public participation route usable without private Harness access. Private resources MUST be clearly labeled and optional for a contribution that does not use the tool.
- Detailed setup, SDD, GitHub delivery and validation instructions MUST remain in their maintained canonical sources. Entry guidance links to them instead of duplicating manuals.
- Missing access or an unavailable replacement target MUST be stated. Preserve the existing usable route until the replacement is verified and separately authorized for activation.

## Provenance, terms and acceptance

Contributors MUST identify original and third-party material, source/version, applicable terms/notices and relevant limitations. Reviewers MUST verify the claimed provenance and permission for the intended contribution/use before acceptance; unresolved rights or required review prevent acceptance.

Harness/plugin users MUST follow the actual approved terms applicable to their tool version. The target project's license and contribution terms remain separate. A new tool-terms draft, file presence or SDD approval does not establish an approved grant.

This Standard creates no CLA, DCO, IP transfer or new contribution-license mechanism. Such a mechanism needs an explicit owner decision and appropriate review. Do not infer authorization from public visibility, successful download or being named in CODEOWNERS.

## Review and delivery

Link an actual issue and approved scope, report the checks actually performed and unavailable verification, and follow [Git](git.md), [Testing](testing.md) and the [SDD process](../../sdd/process.md). Planning approvals, implementation authority, archive preparation, PR review, final checks, merge and release remain distinct.

Apply [Licensing and Publication](licensing-publication.md) for component rights and [Security](security.md) for confidential reports. Keep sensitive details out of public issues. Community guidance MUST have aligned English, Portuguese and Spanish under [Language](language.md); alignment includes permissions, recovery, channel status and approval boundaries.

## Per-repository adoption

Each product records its own owner, concrete project terms, applicable verified reporting route, access limits and adoption evidence. Common guides do not grant private access, distribute a plugin or automatically adopt policy in every repository. Unavailable terms/channel verification remain explicit; safe fallback guidance is not proof of complete adoption.

See the maintained [contribution guide](../../docs/user-docs/guides/contributing.md) and [reporting guide](../../docs/user-docs/guides/security-reporting.md) for examples.
