## 1. Holder, scope and approver

Versat E.A.S., constituted in Paraguay as declared by the owner, is the declared holder of the Versat-authored Harness and its Codex skills plugin. Elvis Luis Zoz (@elviszoz) is the person identified by the owner to approve these terms for the company. Actual component rights and third-party obligations remain subject to the [notices inventory](harness-plugin-notices.md) and rights review; the declaration is not proof of ownership of every included file.

These proposed terms cover the Versat-authored Harness resources delivered through the official Codex skills plugin and the private source repository. Third-party materials retain their applicable terms. The terms of a destination project remain separate and are not changed by using the plugin.

## 2. Proposed installation and use permission

Upon separately approved adoption and official availability, any internal or external person may install and use the official Codex skills plugin without charge to obtain guidance and Versat internal standards when contributing to another repository in the Versat Labs GitHub organization, whether public or private.

This permission is limited to that contribution purpose. It does not provide access to a private repository; the contributor must already have the required authorization. It does not grant a general right to use the Harness for unrelated projects or grant rights in the destination project's material. It does not authorize sale or commercialization of the Harness/plugin. No additional classification of commercial versus non-commercial destination projects is invented here.

## 3. Proposed modification permission

Only Versat team developers with authorized access to the private source repository may modify the Harness/plugin source in that repository under its contribution and review process. Access alone does not supply rights to third-party material. External plugin users receive no permission to modify it under this candidate.

## 4. Proposed restrictions

Users, including Versat developers, may not redistribute, commercialize or fork the Harness/plugin. The owner's stated intent to make the official plugin available for installation and use does not give users permission to republish original or modified copies, resell it or publish forks.

Official distribution is a separate action by Versat E.A.S. under H03, requiring its own authorization, rights review and validated delivery mechanism. This draft does not approve a distribution architecture or third-party redistribution rights. If that delivery mechanism requires permissions beyond the owner's stated decision, resolve them with the owner before distribution; do not silently broaden these clauses.

## 5. Provenance and notices

Contributions must identify source, applicable permissions and required notices. Actual third-party grants and notices must be reviewed for the exact content and distribution mode. Missing rights, incompatible obligations or an unverified component prevent approval of the affected scope. This candidate creates no CLA, DCO or assignment of contribution rights.
