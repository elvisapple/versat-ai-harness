# Product Overview

- Product: [Name]
- Owner: [Accountable person or team]
- Review state: Draft
- Updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]

Use this file as the product context for SDD changes. Replace each prompt with project-specific content, distinguish evidence from hypotheses, and record unknowns with a next validation step. Keep a summary and link here if a topic needs a supporting document.

## One Line Pitch

In one sentence, identify the intended customer, the problem and the value the product offers.

## Detailed Value Proposition

Explain the proposed offering, the benefits customers receive, the expected outcomes and the initial boundaries of the product.

## Problem We Solve

Describe the pain, its frequency and impact, current workarounds, and the evidence that it matters to the intended users.

## Solution Description

Describe the main experience and capabilities, how they address the problem, and the initial scope and exclusions. Distinguish planned capabilities from existing ones.

## Project Origin

Explain how the idea arose, the observations or experiences behind it, and what has been learned or tested so far.

## Customers and Users

Identify target segments, users, buyers and other relevant stakeholders. Explain their contexts, needs and differences; for internal products, identify the teams served.

## Market Size and Revenue Model

Describe the market boundaries and business model. For each estimate below, supply the result, units, geography, time horizon, calculation inputs and formula, assumptions, and dated sources. Mark unavailable estimates explicitly.

### TAM — Total Addressable Market

Estimate the total relevant demand within the stated market definition and explain the method.

### SAM — Serviceable Available Market

Explain which part of TAM the offering can serve given its segment, geography and product constraints.

### SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market

Estimate the realistically reachable share over a stated period, supported by acquisition, adoption and delivery assumptions.

### Take Rate and Calculation Basis

Describe pricing and monetization. Where commissions apply, define the revenue and transaction-volume basis of the take rate and show how it translates volume into revenue. Otherwise explain non-applicability and the relevant pricing or internal value model. Keep customer counts, transaction volume and revenue distinct.

## Why Invest Now?

Explain the timing: customer urgency, market or technology changes, opportunity window, and evidence or assumptions supporting investment now.

## Competitors and Differentiation

Identify direct competitors, indirect alternatives and current workarounds. Explain the differences customers would value, their evidence, and limitations or uncertainties.

## Product Innovation

Describe what is new in the product, experience, technology or business model, relative to which alternatives, and what still needs validation. Do not assume novelty implies customer value.

## Scalability

Describe how adoption, distribution, operations and the technical solution can grow. Identify cost drivers, capacity constraints, dependencies and assumptions to test.

## Social and Environmental Considerations

Describe potential benefits and adverse impacts, affected groups, accessibility and inclusion concerns, resource use, and proportionate mitigation or measurement plans. Distinguish intentions from demonstrated outcomes.

## Open Questions and Validation

Record unresolved questions, their impact, a next action and its owner when known. Identify decisions that block implementation separately from research that can continue after bootstrap.
