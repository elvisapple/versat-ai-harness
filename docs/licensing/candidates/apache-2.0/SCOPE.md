## Permissions and conditions proposed for adoption

The unchanged [official text](LICENSE.txt), including its Appendix, is the legal wording proposed for review. For covered first-party material it permits general and commercial use, modification, redistribution and forks subject to its conditions. Sections 2–4 define the copyright/patent grants and redistribution conditions, including license copies, changed-file notices and retained notices. Section 5 governs intentionally submitted contributions unless explicitly stated otherwise; this candidate adds no separate CLA, DCO or IP assignment. Sections 6–9 retain trademark limits, warranty/liability terms and conditions for additional support obligations. This summary does not modify the license.

The tool license does not determine the license of a destination project merely because the Harness was used. A copied Harness template remains subject to applicable terms for that copied material; unrelated project material retains its own terms. Third-party excerpts, licenses and other material keep their original terms. Public visibility alone grants neither missing third-party rights nor repository write, merge or release authority.

Its holder/provenance and review history remain attributable; its other clauses do not silently amend Apache. `docs/licensing/reviewed/harness-plugin-terms.draft-0.2.md`, the original approval and all archived dossiers remain unchanged. No previous approval transfers to R1.

No root LICENSE/NOTICE is applied by preparing these files. Actual text approval, rights/surface clearance, the future exact adoption patch, license adoption, release/visibility and consumer migration are separate events. Private/public surface findings remain deferred even if this text is approved. The exact patch and final distribution tree require their own review and drift check before use.
