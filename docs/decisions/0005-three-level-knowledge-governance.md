# ADR-0005 — Govern knowledge at three levels

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-28

## Context

Unbounded self-learning can turn local observations into corporate rules and change institutional behavior without review.

## Decision

Classify knowledge into:

1. operational memory, which may be updated automatically within retention policies;
2. Skills, whose evolution requires a proposal and human approval;
3. corporate policies and instructions, whose evolution requires a branch, PR, review and checks.

## Consequences

- learning remains possible without sacrificing governance;
- agents must indicate the affected knowledge level;
- promoting memory to a Skill or policy leaves an audit trail.
